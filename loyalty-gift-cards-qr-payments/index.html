FAQ

Loyalty, gift cards & QR, answered

Will loyalty and gift cards work with my POS?

In most cases, yes. We match programs that integrate with Clover, Lightspeed, TouchBistro, Odoo and other popular POS systems so points and balances update at checkout.

How long do custom plastic gift cards take?

Timing depends on design approval and quantity. We send a digital proof first, and you approve before anything is printed.

Is QR scan-to-pay secure?

Yes. The QR opens a secure, encrypted checkout hosted by the payment provider. Customers pay with Apple Pay, Google Pay or card on their own device.

Can I accept tap payments on my phone?

Tap to pay on a phone is available on compatible devices through supported providers. We'll check your device and set it up if it fits.
