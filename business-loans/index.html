Funding options

The right type of capital for the job.

Each product solves a different problem. Not sure which fits? Tell us what the money is for and we'll recommend the best structure.

Most popular

Merchant Cash Advance

Get a lump sum today, repay automatically as a small share of future card sales.

  • Based on card sales history — not just credit score
  • Repayment flexes with your sales: slow day, smaller payment
  • No fixed monthly installment
  • Use for anything: payroll, renovations, stock

Great for: Restaurants, retail, salons with steady card volume

Business Cash Receivable Loan

Turn unpaid invoices into working capital — stop waiting 30–90 days to get paid.

  • Advance against outstanding invoices / receivables
  • Funding grows as your sales grow
  • Smooths cash-flow gaps from slow-paying clients
  • Keep operations and payroll on schedule

Great for: B2B, trades, wholesale, contractors, staffing

Inventory Loan

Stock up before peak season or seize bulk-buy discounts without draining cash.

  • Finance purchases of inventory & raw materials
  • Inventory can help secure the facility
  • Buy at volume pricing, sell at full margin
  • Ideal pre-holiday and seasonal top-ups

Great for: Retail, eCommerce, distributors, grocers

Business Expansion Loan

Open a new location, renovate, hire, or launch a new product line.

  • Term loans structured to your growth plan
  • Fund leaseholds, fit-out, hiring & marketing
  • Predictable payments for budgeting
  • Pair with grants to stretch every dollar

Great for: Established businesses ready to scale

Equipment Loan & Leasing

Get the equipment you need now and pay over its useful life.

  • Kitchen, manufacturing, medical, vehicles & tech
  • The equipment itself secures the financing
  • Preserve cash and credit lines
  • Lease-to-own and upgrade options

Great for: Restaurants, clinics, trades, manufacturers

Business Line of Credit

A flexible safety net — draw what you need, pay interest only on what you use.

  • Revolving access to capital
  • Cover payroll, gaps & surprise costs
  • Re-borrow as you repay
  • Great companion to a cash advance

Great for: Any business with uneven cash flow
