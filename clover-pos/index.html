The Clover family

Mix and match devices that work as one system.

Start with one device and add more as you grow — every Clover device shares the same menu, inventory, staff and reporting.

Clover Flex

Portable, full-featured POS for tableside, curbside and on-the-go.

  • Built-in receipt printer & barcode scanner
  • Wi-Fi + LTE connectivity
  • All-day battery for a typical shift
  • Tipping, signatures & split payments

Clover Flex Pocket

The slimmest Flex — digital-only receipts for pop-ups & services.

  • Pocket-sized, no printer to maintain
  • Receipts by text or email
  • Wi-Fi + LTE
  • Tap, chip & swipe

Clover Mini

Compact countertop POS that fits any checkout.

  • 8" touchscreen
  • Wi-Fi, Ethernet & LTE options
  • Built-in printer
  • Use alone or as a Station Duo customer display

Clover Station Duo

Two screens, one powerful hub for busy counters.

  • 14" merchant + 8" customer screen
  • Printer, cash drawer, scanner support
  • Customer tips, loyalty & PIN entry
  • Best for QSR, retail & salons

Clover Station Solo

Single-screen countertop station for full-service POS.

  • Large HD merchant display
  • High-speed receipt printer
  • Inventory & employee management
  • Add a Mini or Flex as you grow

Clover Kiosk

Self-serve ordering that shortens lines and lifts ticket size.

  • Customers order & pay themselves
  • Upsell prompts on every order
  • Sends orders straight to the kitchen
  • Frees staff for service
