FAQ

Retail payments, answered

What is the best POS for a small retail store?

Clover Mini or Station Duo on the Register plan is a great value for single stores. Lightspeed Retail and Odoo are ideal when you need advanced inventory, purchase orders, multiple stores or a synced online shop.

Can my online store and in-store inventory stay in sync?

Yes. Lightspeed, Odoo and Clover (with integrations) can sync stock between your POS and eCommerce so you never oversell.

How can I lower my card processing costs?

Start with a free statement review. We look at your effective rate, pricing model (flat vs interchange-plus), Interac debit fees and any monthly or equipment-lease charges.

Can I get financing for inventory?

Yes — inventory loans and lines of credit help you stock up before busy seasons without draining cash.
