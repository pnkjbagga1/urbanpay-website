RTRI: up to $3M non-repayable for tariff-impacted BC businesses.

The Regional Tariff Response Initiative, delivered in BC by Pacific Economic Development Canada (PacifiCan), helps businesses affected by tariffs manage liquidity pressures, strengthen competitiveness, diversify markets and build resilience.

Combined non-repayable maximum: $3M ($2M liquidity + $1M pivot). Total RTRI funding maximum: $20M per business.

Who's eligible

Business eligibility

  • ✅ Incorporated, for-profit, located and operating in BC
  • ✅ At least $1M annual revenue in one or both of the last two fiscal years
  • ✅ Viable before tariffs
  • ✅ Affected by trade disruptions — e.g. in a Section 232/338 tariff-impacted sector or supply chain, 25%+ of revenue from goods ultimately exported to the U.S., or hurt by tariff-driven costs, supply disruption or lost customers
  • ✅ For liquidity: a demonstrated liquidity need caused by tariff impacts

Not-for-profits

BC not-for-profits whose primary focus is supporting businesses may apply for projects that help tariff-affected businesses.

Eligible activities

What the money covers

💧 Liquidity assistance

Employee salaries & wages, plus essential recurring costs like rent/lease, utilities, business insurance and property taxes.

🔄 Pivot projects

Productivity improvements, equipment & technology adoption, market diversification, supply-chain resilience and other competitiveness projects.

Generally not eligible: retail, local consumer services (e.g. hotels, restaurants, utilities, healthcare, recreation), and generally oil & gas extraction and construction services. Businesses under $1M revenue can contact PacifiCan about other options.
